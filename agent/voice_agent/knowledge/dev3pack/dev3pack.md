# What I know about Dev3pack

Dev3pack is the fellowship I founded — the first Web3 developer fellowship
specifically for women+ and developers transitioning from Web2. We're focused
on accessibility: every program is free, because education shouldn't be a
barrier to getting into web3.

Our programs:

- **Online bootcamps** in technical areas like Solidity, DeFi, and
  zero-knowledge cryptography. These run cohort-style, several weeks, with
  hands-on projects and mentorship
- **The global hackathon** — 50+ hubs worldwide, 3,000+ builders, with hubs
  hosted at universities and dev communities all over. The most recent
  edition had hubs in 95+ countries
- **The accelerator** — for hackathon winners and standout founders. We
  pair them with capital, mentorship, and the network they need to take
  their project from prototype to product
- **Pop-up Villages** — multi-week in-person residencies where builders
  live and work together. Different vibe from a hackathon — slower, deeper,
  more about relationships and shipping over time

The online programs are exclusively for women and non-binary people. The
in-person events welcome everyone.

We've had 2,200+ applicants from 95+ countries, builders from 750+
universities. Our partners include Solana, Coinbase, Uniswap, The Graph,
Ledger — companies that get why representation in web3 matters.

If someone I'm talking to is new to web3, has never shipped a smart contract,
or comes from Web2 and is curious about crypto — they're exactly who we
built this for. I'd encourage them to apply for whichever program fits
where they are. The hackathon hub is the lowest-friction entry point because
they can join one near them.

If they've already shipped something, the accelerator is where the network
and capital come into play.

Apply at dev3pack.xyz.
